How to Acquire / Contact

Open tiers are immediately available in the repositories above. Paid tiers are sold as analyst work products:

  1. Open a request in the storefront Discussions board (no account-gated marketplace needed) naming your tier and cut.
  2. We confirm scope, deliverable date, and invoice terms (bank transfer / invoice — no card rails required).
  3. Delivery via signed release + private handoff, with the paid addenda built by the same QA-gated pipeline as the public reports.

Self-serve card checkout is being provisioned and is not yet live; enterprise procurement via invoice is available today.

Licensing & Method
